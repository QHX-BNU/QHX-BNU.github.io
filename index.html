03 Research
ACL 2026

One LLM Does Not Simulate All Students

Ability-Aware Student Simulation via Cognitive Diagnosis Guided LLM Assignment

Large Language Models have become integral to personalized education systems, yet most methods rely on a single high-capability LLM to represent an entire population of diverse learners. We demonstrate that this "one-size-fits-all" approach induces a systematic ability-dependent bias, and propose a framework that dynamically matches students with appropriate LLM backbones through cognitive alignment via Neural Cognitive Diagnosis.
